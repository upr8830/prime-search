**No—that is no longer true.** Medicare removed the "three injections per day" requirement in 2023.

As of April 16, 2023, Medicare's updated criteria removed the language requiring beneficiaries to be "insulin-treated with multiple (three or more) daily administrations" to qualify for a CGM. Current Medicare coverage standards generally require only that you:

- Been diagnosed with diabetes, **and**
- Are treated with insulin (any insulin regimen, including fewer than three injections or a pump), **or** 
- Have a documented history of "problematic hypoglycemia" (recurrent severe low blood sugars) even if not on insulin.
