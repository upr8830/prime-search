For Medicare to cover a Continuous Glucose Monitor (CGM), the following practitioner visit requirements must be met:

## Initial Coverage Visit
As part of the **initial provision** of a CGM, the treating practitioner must conduct an **in-person or Medicare-approved telehealth visit** with the beneficiary. The medical record from that visit must document that:
1. The beneficiary has **diabetes mellitus**
2. The beneficiary (or caregiver) has received **appropriate training** in the use of the CGM, evidenced by a prescription
3. The CGM is prescribed in accordance with **FDA indications for use**
4. The CGM is prescribed to improve glycemic control for a beneficiary who is **insulin-treated** or has a **history of problematic hypoglycemia**

## Continued Coverage Visit
To justify **continued coverage**, the treating practitioner must conduct an **in-person or Medicare-approved telehealth visit with the beneficiary every 6 months** following the initial prescription. The visit must document:
- The beneficiary's **adherence to their CGM regimen and diabetes treatment plan**
- That the supplies remain **medically necessary**

**Note:** There is no requirement for the practitioner to reconfirm that the initial coverage criteria are met for continued coverage—only the 6-month adherence visit is required.

Failure to document this 6-month visit can result in insufficient documentation errors and payment recoupment.
