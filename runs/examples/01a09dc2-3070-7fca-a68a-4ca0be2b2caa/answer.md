## Answer
No. As of the April 2023 revision—reflected in LCD L33822 (effective 2024‑10‑01) and Article A52464 (effective 2025‑02‑18)—Medicare no longer requires three or more insulin injections per day for CGM coverage; it requires only that the beneficiary be insulin‑treated (or have a history of problematic hypoglycemia) without specifying a minimum number of daily insulin administrations.

## Criteria / Details
- LCD L33822 – Glucose Monitors (L33822), revision effective 2024‑10‑01: current CGM coverage criterion states “The beneficiary is insulin‑treated;” with no minimum number of daily insulin administrations specified. [11] 
- LCD L33822 – Glucose Monitors (L33822), revision effective 2024‑10‑01: the April 2023 revision removed the requirement for “multiple (three or more) daily administrations of insulin or a continuous subcutaneous insulin infusion (CSII) pump” from the CGM coverage criterion. [5] 
- Article A52464 – Glucose Monitor – Policy Article (A52464), revision effective 2025‑02‑18: CGM coverage requires the beneficiary to be insulin treated or have a history of problematic hypoglycemia, with no specified number of daily insulin injections. [9] 
- Article A52464 – Glucose Monitor – Policy Article (A52464), revision effective 2025‑02‑18: the April 2023 revision removed language from criterion 1 regarding frequent dosing of insulin. [10] 
- For billing, Article A52464 requires modifier KX when the beneficiary is insulin‑treated. [6]

## Codes and documentation
- Modifier KX must be used on CGM claims for insulin‑treated beneficiaries per Article A52464 (effective 2025‑02‑18). [6]

## Effective dates relied on
- LCD L33822 – Glucose Monitors (L33822) – CMS, revision effective 2024‑10‑01 
- Article A52464 – Glucose Monitor – Policy Article (A52464) – CMS, revision effective 2025‑02‑18 

## Contradictions and caveats
Non‑CMS sources such as DiabeticSuppliesHub ([2]) and Aeroflow Diabetes ([4], [7]) state that three daily insulin injections are required (or not required) for Medicare CGM coverage. These secondary statements are overridden by the primary policy documents LCD L33822 (effective 2024‑10‑01) and Article A52464 (effective 2025‑02‑18), which specify that coverage requires insulin treatment without a minimum number of daily insulin injections.
