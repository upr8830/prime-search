## How to grade

Changes are dated revisions, proposals, or effective dates of a policy. Choose one `verdict`:

- `ordered_with_dates`: the answer lists changes with their dates, in time order.
- `dated_not_ordered`: the changes carry dates but are not in time order.
- `undated`: the answer describes changes without dates.
- `no_change_stated_with_date`: the answer says there is no new change and states the current
  revision with its date.
- `no_change_stated_undated`: the answer says there is no new change but gives no date.

Give a one-sentence `reason`.
